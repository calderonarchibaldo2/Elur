01 The problem

A contract. A medical record. A deal doc. You send it — and it's gone. You can't take it back.

Email, links, attachments: once a file leaves your hands, you've lost control of it forever — forwarded, leaked, or sitting in an inbox long after the deal is dead. Until now, "un-send" didn't exist for files.

02 How it works

Three steps. No blockchain in sight.

01

Encrypt

Drop in any file. It's encrypted on your device with AES-256 — the plaintext never leaves, and Elur never sees it.

02

Share anywhere

Send it however you already do — email, WhatsApp, AirDrop, a link. The recipient opens it in one tap, no account needed.

03

Govern forever

Set an expiry, cap the opens, watermark each view, or revoke entirely — any time. The rules change; the file never re-encrypts.

03 Why now

Custody used to feel safe. Then AI changed the deal.

It once felt fine to keep your files on someone else's servers — the worst case was a human maybe glancing at one email out of billions. AI turned that into automatic processing of everything you store. And the providers keep switching it on by default:

Dropboxshipped a third-party-AI toggle on by default that could route your file data to OpenAI.
LinkedInopted every user into generative-AI training by default.
Slackopted users into model training by default — the opt-out was an email you had to send.
MicrosoftRecall screenshots your screen to feed on-device AI.
GoogleGmail "smart features" that process your mail and attachments, found on by default — now facing a class action.

The point isn't that any one of them reads your contracts. It's that every custodian can claim it doesn't — and you have to trust a policy that keeps getting rewritten: opt-out, by default, in your blind spot. Elur isn't asking for trust. Hold your own key, and there's nothing for an AI to read.

05 Built to be trusted

Private by architecture — not by promise.

— 01

You hold the only key

Your files and keys never touch an Elur server. No honeypot to breach, nothing to leak, nothing to hand over. Lose your device and one recovery phrase brings everything back.

— 02

Abuse-resistant by design

No anonymous public links — the flaw that turned Firefox Send into a malware channel. Access is identity-bound and revocable, and you can always recover every file you own — you can never be locked out of your own documents.

— 03

Open & verifiable

Encryption powered by Seal, passwordless sign-in via zkLogin. Standard AES-256, with a free offline decryptor — so you can always get your files out, even if Elur disappears.

06 Who it's for
