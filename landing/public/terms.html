01What Elur is

Elur is software for governed, revocable access to encrypted documents. It is non-custodial: encryption happens on your device, your files travel on public infrastructure (Walrus), and access is enforced by a public gate (Sui + Seal). Elur never possesses your files or your keys. We provide the software and, on some plans, sponsor your network transactions.

02Preview status

Elur currently runs on test networks. It is offered for evaluation, not for production secrets. Test networks can reset, and testnet data may be lost. We will say clearly when that changes.

03Your keys, your responsibility

Your recovery phrase is the only way to restore your access. We do not have it, cannot reset it, and cannot recover documents for you if you lose it. Write it down and keep it safe. This is the price of a provider that cannot read your files, and it is not negotiable by design.

04The ledger is permanent

Grants, revocations, and opens are recorded on a public blockchain. These records are pseudonymous, permanent, and cannot be edited or deleted — by you, by your counterparties, or by us. Do not use Elur if you require erasable access records.

05What revocation does — and doesn't

Revoking access prevents every future open, immediately and everywhere. It cannot reach copies, screenshots, or knowledge already extracted while access was valid. Retaining or using content after revocation may constitute misappropriation, and the ledger provides evidence of the access lease — but enforcement against bad actors is a matter of law, not software.
